Questions.

How do you turn a photo into a video on iPhone?

Open Photo to Video, pick a photo, describe the motion you want, choose a model, and generate. There is no timeline and no editing experience required — the AI produces the movement.

Is there a subscription?

No. You buy credits only when you generate something, and they never expire. There is no monthly or annual charge.

Can it animate between two different photos?

Yes. Transition mode takes a first and last frame and generates the motion between them.

Can a character copy the movement from another video?

Yes. Motion Control takes a character image plus a reference video, and the character performs the same movement.

Which AI models does it use?

Fifteen, including Sora 2, Veo 3.1, Seedance 2.0, Kling 3, Wan 2.7 and Vidu Q3. You pick the model per generation, at no extra cost and with no separate account.

Do you need an account?

Not to start. You can install the app and create straight away; signing in is only required to buy credits.

What happens to the photos you upload?

They are sent to our AI provider solely to generate the result you asked for, after you explicitly agree in the app. Nothing is used to train models, and the app performs no facial recognition and creates no faceprint.

Is there an Android or web version?

No. Photo to Video is iPhone-only.
