How to turn a photo into a video with AI

Pick a photo in the Photo to Video app for iPhone and the AI generates a short video of it moving — a portrait that turns and blinks, a landscape whose water and clouds drift. A written prompt is optional: with no prompt the model infers plausible motion from the image itself, and with one you direct what moves. Ten models are available, clips run 1 to 20 seconds, and the highest resolution is 4K.

Steps

  1. Open the app. Photo to Video opens on this mode — it is the home screen.
  2. Pick a photo. Choose any image from your camera roll.
  3. Optionally describe the motion. Leave it blank to let the model decide, or write what should move.
  4. Choose a model, length and resolution. Ten models support this mode; see the table below.
  5. Generate and save. Save the finished clip to your camera roll.

Which model to use

Seedance 1.0 Pro is the default and the cheapest per second — the right first try for any photo. Veo 3.1 is the one to pick when you need 4K or synchronised audio. Sora 2 and Seedance 2.0 handle complex scenes and camera movement most convincingly, at a higher cost per second. Hailuo-02 and Grok Imagine are quick, cheaper options for simple subject motion.

Getting a better result

  • A clear single subject animates far better than a busy scene. Crop before you generate.
  • Describe movement, not appearance. “Her hair lifts in the wind” gives the model something to do; “beautiful photo” does not.
  • Start at 4–5 seconds. It costs a fraction of a 20-second clip and tells you whether the prompt works.
  • If one model refuses an image, try another — each has its own content rules and switching is free.

Questions

Can AI animate a still photo?

Yes. Given one image it generates frames of that scene in motion, rather than panning or zooming across the original.

Do you need to write a prompt?

No. For this mode the prompt is optional — without one the model infers motion from the image. A prompt gives you control over what moves.

How long can the video be?

From 1 to 20 seconds depending on the model. Sora 2 and Seedance 2.0 allow the longest clips; Veo 3.1 is fixed at 4, 6 or 8 seconds.

Can it produce 4K?

Yes, with Veo 3.1. The other models top out at 1080p.

Is it free?

The app is free to install and there is no subscription. Generating uses credits, which you buy in one-time packs and which never expire.
