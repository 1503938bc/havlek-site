Our Story

Built on real enterprise experience

Havlek was founded by Henry Zou, a full-stack developer and digital marketer with over a decade of experience delivering enterprise-grade technology solutions across Canada, the US, and internationally.

What started as a web development and digital marketing practice grew into an engineering firm trusted with systems that run real businesses. We've built e-commerce platforms for national retailers, cloud infrastructure for telecom providers, mobile apps for international publishers, and dispatch systems for national service companies.

Today we work as Forward Deployed Engineers: we embed with your team, build inside the systems you already run, and stay until the AI is in production. We proved the approach on ourselves first, turning a decade of web-building know-how into Done4Me, an AI employee that builds and maintains small-business websites.
