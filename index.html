How an engagement runs

Embed, ship, harden, hand over

01 — Embed

Work alongside your team

We sit with the people doing the work, get access to the real systems and data, and pick the one problem worth solving first.

02 — Ship

Put a working version in their hands

We build inside your stack and ship early, so your team uses it and tells us what's wrong while it's still cheap to change.

03 — Harden

Make it reliable

Evaluations on real cases, monitoring, cost controls and guardrails, so it keeps working after launch, not just in the demo.

04 — Hand over

Your team owns it

Documentation, training and a clean handover. We stay on call to extend it as the next problem comes into view.
