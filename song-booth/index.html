Questions.

How do you make a song with AI on iPhone?

Open Song Booth, describe the song in a sentence, pick a model, and create. About a minute later a finished recording comes back with words, a voice and an arrangement. No instrument, no editor and no musical training is involved.

Do you have to write the lyrics yourself?

No. Simple mode writes them from your description. If you would rather control them, Custom mode takes your own lyrics and style, and AI Lyrics will draft a set from a brief and rewrite them as many times as you want.

Which AI music models does Song Booth use?

Seven, across Suno and Mureka: Suno V5.5, V5, V4.5 and V4, and Mureka V9, V8 and V7.6. You choose per song, with a note on each saying what it is for, and there is no separate account or extra charge for any of them.

Is there a subscription?

Your first song is free. After that Song Booth Pro is $5.99 a week or $59.99 a year, which unlocks the Pro models and unlimited songs on the standard ones. Listening to songs you have already made is never metered.

Can you play your songs offline?

Yes. A song is kept on the device once it has been played, and the library works with no network. Songs also play in the background, on the lock screen, over AirPlay and through CarPlay, and can be grouped into albums.

Can you send a song to somebody who does not have the app?

Yes. Every song has a link that opens a web page with the recording and its lyrics. Whoever you send it to can listen without installing anything.

Do you need an account?

No. There is nothing to create and no email to hand over. Songs are kept for you, follow you to a new phone, and can be erased completely whenever you want.

Is there an Android or web version?

No. Song Booth is iPhone-only, and needs iOS 15 or later.
